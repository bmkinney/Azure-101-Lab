# Post-Lab Feedback Questionnaire

Use this questionnaire to collect student feedback after the Azure 101 Operations Lab. Questions map to the learning objectives and modules in the [student guide](student-guide.md).

**Suggested delivery:** Microsoft Forms or Google Forms. Send the link in the last 10 minutes of the lab while the experience is fresh, with a reminder at lab close and 24 hours later. Target completion time: 10–15 minutes.

A machine-readable column template is also provided in [`feedback-template.csv`](feedback-template.csv) for importing into a form builder or analyzing exported responses.

---

## Section 1 — Overall experience

*5-point Likert scale: Strongly disagree / Disagree / Neutral / Agree / Strongly agree*

1. The lab met the stated learning objectives.
2. The difficulty level was appropriate for my experience.
3. The pace of the lab was appropriate (neither rushed nor dragging).
4. The balance between hands-on work and instruction was right.
5. I feel more confident operating Azure environments after this lab.
6. I would recommend this lab to a colleague.

10. Was the group size of 3 students per subscription the right size for collaboration? *(Too small / Just right / Too large)*
